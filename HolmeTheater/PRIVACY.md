# Holme Theater Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Holme Theater is a client for Jellyfin, media server software that you or someone you know runs. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the developer of Holme Theater doesn't collect any of it.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. Holme Theater has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and it doesn't send any information to the developer or to any third party.

## What the app sends to your Jellyfin server

To work, the app communicates only with the Jellyfin server you sign in to. It sends that server:

- Your username and password, or a Quick Connect code, to sign you in
- The app's name and version, and a name and random identifier for your device, so the server can list it among your devices
- What you play and how far you get, so your progress and Continue Watching stay in sync
- Changes you make, such as marking items watched, adding favorites, and editing playlists or collections

Your Jellyfin server is operated by you or by whoever runs it, not by the developer of Holme Theater. How that server stores and uses this information is governed by its operator and by Jellyfin's own software.

## What's stored on your device

- Your sign-in session, kept in the system keychain
- Your settings, such as home screen layout and themes
- Artwork cached to make browsing faster
- Any videos you download for offline viewing

Signing out removes your session, cached artwork, and your downloads. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Links to other websites

Some pages link to websites such as IMDb and The Movie Database. They open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

## Children

Holme Theater doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
